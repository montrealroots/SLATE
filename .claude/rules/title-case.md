# Title Case on screens (the owner's rule, asked for on 2026-10-29)

When screens are built (`docs/BUILD-ORDER.md` layer 5), every label the player reads on a screen is in Title Case:
buttons, headings, tabs, menu items, field labels and choices, every word capitalized, short words included, as the
owner's documents write them (Same As Your Place Of Birth, Add A Language, Guide Me Through It, Zoom In, In Your Own
Words, I Know SLATE). Sentences the game says (notices, explanations, the catalog's lines that read as sentences) stay
sentences.

The rule isn't written in `docs/`; the owner asked that it be picked up, and its form here is read from how
`docs/KEEP.md` and `docs/SCREENS.md` write the labels. Where the owner words it otherwise, the owner's wording holds.
When the text catalog gains its first label, the gate's text check is to hold labels to it.
