# The New SLATE, Screen By Screen, As Decided So Far

The screens of the new build in the order the player meets them, each as a full page, drawn from the register's
decisions up to entry 11 (2026-10-26). What's on a page is only what survived the owner's cuts: no Yale, nothing of
the School, nothing of this SLATE's career engine, no attributes, no decoration. Where a thing on a page is still
open, it says so. Pages past the last reviewed one are listed by name only.

---

## Screen 1. The startup screen

Kept whole (register, entry 1). Full dark field with the radial glow at the center, the clapper mark arriving with
its 900 ms turn, **SLATE** in the spaced light wordmark, "A LIFE SIMULATOR" under it. A hairline gold progress bar,
the current stage's name on the left and the percentage on the right, and the four stages listed under it, each
ticking from a dot to a gold arrow to a check: Connecting To Storage, Reading Save Slots, Checking Each Save,
Checking Save Versions. "Version 1.1.0 · Save Format 94" at the foot (the numbers become the new build's own). If a
stage fails, the status reads "Startup Stopped", the stage shows a red cross, and a red box says what failed, why,
and offers Try Again. If a save needs a word (from a newer or older version, or unreadable), a gold box "Before You
Continue" lists it and waits for Continue. Otherwise it goes straight on.

## Screen 2. The slots screen

Kept whole in feature and engine (entry 2); every word on it to be rewritten later in the owner's voice, natural,
coherent and realistic. The clapper and wordmark at the top, one line under them, then three cards across (one above
another on a phone).

- An empty card: dashed border, the slot number, one line, and the button to start a life.
- A card with a life in it: the headshot if one was given, the name in large type, and a line under the name
  **to be redefined**: it read "Actress, Yale Drama Year 1" and that's gone with the School; the obvious
  replacement is age and where they live, settled with the premise. Then four facts in two columns: the day, where,
  worth, time played. A small line with the version the life was made with (and a note if it'll be brought up to
  date on opening, and its size). A gold full-width button to continue; under it, side by side, Bring Back The Copy
  (when a copy exists, with the copy's date under it) and the delete button.
- A finished life's main button reads differently and opens on how it ended.
- A life that can't be read: "Unreadable" in place of the name, the reason in red, the copy button if there's a
  copy, delete.
- Delete opens a dimmed modal naming the person, saying what goes with them and whether a copy will remain, with
  Keep and Delete.
- A foot line: the version and where lives are kept (this device, or the server, which checks each one).

## Screen 3. Creation, Identity

The first of the creation steps. Header "Who Are You Going To Be?" with its one-line premise (to be rewritten with
the premise), Cancel at the right, and the step strip across the top. The steps are now **Identity, Background,
Genres, Starting Point, Review** (Dispositions & Traits is gone; Genres & Background split into two steps). One tall card, in
this order, top to bottom (entry 3):

1. **Your face and your name.** The portrait frame on the left (drop a photo, any size, cropped to a headshot;
   its reader in the new build is open, since the headshot's career reader is gone; it keeps the Profile and the
   slot card). On the right: First Name, Middle Name (optional), Last Name; Preferred Name; Pronouns; Billed As (Actor or
   Actress), kept with an engine to come, like race or ethnicity.
2. **When you were born.** Year, Month, Day, and directly under them the line that says how old you'll be when you
   start.
3. **Where you were born.** The world map (hover a country, Zoom In, Hide Map), then Country, State Or Region, and
   the town search over the whole gazetteer (252 countries and territories, 170,911 places), the matching towns
   listed with their populations.
4. **Citizenship.** The country list, with Same As Your Place Of Birth offered once a birthplace is chosen. Reads
   into the passport, visas, work permission where that exists, tax, and which banks will open an account.
5. **Languages.** English with how well it's spoken, Add A Language (up to five more, each with Native, Fluent or
   Conversational), the fluency legend. Suggested from the birthplace's country in the new build.
6. **Race Or Ethnicity**, last: the eight to press, and In Your Own Words under Another. Optional. Its reader in the
   new build is open (the casting reader went with the career); under the zero-decoration rule it stays only if a
   reader is named with the premise.
7. The amber box listing what the step still needs, and Back / Continue.

## Screen 4. Creation, Background

Three cards in the order a life happens (entries 9 and 11):

1. **The family you were born into.** "The Family You Come From", six to choose: Nothing, Scraping By, Modest,
   Comfortable, Wealthy, Inherited. Under it a box in the player's money saying exactly what that gives on the
   first morning: checking, savings, the card's limit and what's owed on it, the credit score. (The School's half of
   the old box, what Yale found and the Yale Health fee, is gone.)
2. **What your family did.** A structured choice (the list is to be written with the premise), read by the means
   above and by what the trade leaves a person with.
3. **Where you grew up.** "The same as where you were born", or the place picker again. Sets the starting accent
   and refines the languages Identity suggested.

Back / Continue at the foot. Nothing else: no genres (moved off, destination open), no free-text sentence, no
languages (on Identity), no training, no prior work, no arrival date.

## Screen 5. Creation, Starting Point

One card, **Your Money** (entry 14): the line that the money is kept in the currency where the life starts and that
exchange rates are estimates; Your Bank, a real bank with its real fee line; and the figures the family set, checking,
savings, credit record, the card's limit. Nothing else: the body, the town facts and the earlier residence are cut.
Where the life begins on the first morning is open until the premise.

## Screen 6. Creation, Review

Ready To Begin with the name and a line under it (age, billed as, home, the last open until the premise). Four
sections in the steps' order: Identity, Background, Genres, Money, each saying back what its step took. The paused-clock line. Back and Begin. (The Guide card is gone with the tutorial, entry 16.) The School line, the dispositions, the free-text sentence, Where You
Live and Food Each Month are gone (entry 15).

## Screen 7. Home, the first morning

No tutorial (entry 16): nothing locked, no guide line, no chapter count. The top bar (the clock, Paused, Play, Sleep (held), Advance,
Saved, Save & Exit; the Guide button and the How This Works links are gone, entry 19), the menu down the left (Player Profile, Money, Papers), the greeting with the
date and the town's line, the weather and its five-day forecast, and the panels What Needs You, Up Next with the
calendar, and Recent Updates. How You Are and Look After Yourself are gone with their engines (entry 18). The School and Career menu entries, the term's events, the School's welcome notice and the readiness meter are gone
with their engines (entry 17); the rest stands with its engine until the owner rules on it, panel by panel.
